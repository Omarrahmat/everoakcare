Complaints and comments

Tell us if something isn't right

Anyone can raise a concern or complaint: a child, a parent, a social worker, a professional or a neighbour. Complaints help us improve. No one is ever treated differently for complaining.

If your complaint is about a child's safety, please read our safeguarding page. Safety concerns are acted on the same day.

How to complain

  1. Speak to us. Most worries can be sorted out quickly. Call 07342 045712 or email omar@everoakcare.com.
  2. Formal complaint. If you are not happy with the answer, put your complaint in writing to the same email. We acknowledge it within 3 working days and give a written response within 10 working days.
  3. Independent review. If you are still not satisfied, ask for a review by our Responsible Individual, who was not involved. We respond within 20 working days.

Children's complaints

Children can tell any member of staff, their keyworker, their social worker or an independent advocate. We help them complain in the way that suits them: words, pictures, symbols, signs or a device. Children are told how to complain in our easy-read Children's Guide.
