Our promise

Rooted in belonging.

Our oak tree is more than a logo. It is our promise. Like an oak, we stand firm as a sheltering, rooted home for children and young people who have known too much uncertainty.

Belonging sits at the heart of the tree, and every other value grows from it.

We don't just provide a place to stay. We build secure, lasting homes where young people feel valued and supported to grow into their full potential.

Who we care for

Children who need a home that fits the way they think, feel and communicate.

Our Statement of Purpose sets out exactly who each home cares for. We only accept a placement when we are confident we can meet the child's needs alongside the young people already living there.

Learning disabilities

Clear, visual routines, life skills at each child's pace and patient support with school, health and family time.

Autism

Predictable days, low-arousal spaces and adults who learn each child's communication: spoken, signed, symbols or AAC.

Sensory processing needs

Every child has a sensory profile that shapes their room, their day and how we help them regulate.

Emotional wellbeing

Trauma-informed, relationship-based care for children who have had a difficult start.

Our approach

Roots, routine and room to grow.

  1. 01

    Get to know the child

    Before moving in: visits, a sensory profile, a communication passport and a transition plan built with the child, family and social worker.

  2. 02

    Consistent adults

    A small, stable team and a named keyworker, trained in autism, positive behaviour support and safeguarding before their first shift.

  3. 03

    Joined-up support

    We work closely with schools, CAMHS, speech and language and OT so the plan is the same at home and in class.

  4. 04

    Their voice counts

    Children shape their home and their plans, in whatever way they communicate best.

Referrals and commissioning

We respond to every referral the same working day with an honest view on whether we can meet the child's needs. If we can't, we'll say so quickly.
