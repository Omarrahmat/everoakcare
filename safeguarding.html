Safeguarding

Keeping children safe

Every child at Everoak Care has the right to be safe and to be heard, however they communicate. Safeguarding is everyone's responsibility, and every adult who works with us acts on any concern the same day.

If a child is in immediate danger, call 999.

How to report a concern

If you are worried about a child living with us, please tell us straight away. You can also go directly to the local authority or the police.

Concerns about a member of staff

Any concern that an adult working with children may have caused harm is referred to the Local Authority Designated Officer (LADO) within one working day. You can contact Croydon's LADO directly at LADO@croydon.gov.uk or 020 8726 6000 (extension 24817).

Our approach

  • Safer recruitment: enhanced DBS checks, full employment history and references for every member of staff.
  • Training for all staff in safeguarding, exploitation, online safety and recognising distress in children who communicate without speech.
  • Each child has a communication passport so staff understand how they show they are worried or unhappy.
  • Independent visits every month and Ofsted inspection once we are registered.
  • We work with the London Safeguarding Children Procedures and the Croydon Safeguarding Children Partnership.

Whistleblowing

Staff can raise concerns about practice without fear. Concerns can also go directly to Ofsted's whistleblowing line on 0300 123 3155 or whistleblowing@ofsted.gov.uk, or the NSPCC Whistleblowing Advice Line on 0800 028 0285.

Our full Safeguarding and Child Protection Policy is available to placing authorities and families on request.
